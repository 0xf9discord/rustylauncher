Ferris source: https://rustacean.net/assets/rustacean-flat-happy.svg
Author: Karen Rustad Tölva. Ferris is dedicated to the public domain (CC0), as stated at https://rustacean.net/.

The grass block is an original vector drawing inspired by Minecraft's grass block.
The combined logo is an unofficial project mark, not an official Minecraft logo.

`logo.svg` is the editable master. `logo.png` and `app.ico` are derived exports.
Run `python assets/generate_icons.py` to regenerate all three from the vector geometry and `ferris.svg`.
